The water surface is modeled above a 48-inch sluice bed; moss is shown only between the first and last riffles. Yellow = gold, purple = black sand, light blue = sand. Particles can settle, bounce, circulate, and be remobilized.

Model limits: The mat retention and release coefficients are illustrative, not measured for your equipment. This is a 2D illustrative, reduced-order flow/particle model, not a numerical Navier–Stokes free-surface CFD solver. Recovery percentages are uncalibrated model estimates, not validated recovery efficiency. Real accuracy needs measured discharge, slope, depth, riffle geometry, sediment distribution and field/lab calibration.
